Hi, I'm SeokYeong Lee 👋

I am a postdoctoral researcher at KAIST AI. I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from Korea University in 2026, advised by Prof. Seungryong Kim and Prof. Paul Hongsuck Seo, through the academy–industry program with the Korea Institute of Science and Technology (KIST), where I was also advised by Prof. Ig-Jae Kim.

My research focuses on robust 3D perception under unconstrained conditions: few input views, changing illumination, noisy camera poses, and no 3D labels. I have worked on few-shot neural radiance fields, scene-level inverse rendering (decomposing a scene into geometry, material and lighting), and label-free monocular 3D object detection and tracking.
